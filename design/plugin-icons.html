STAR/FLUX / bar icons

Ten marks for the Omarchy bar widget, replacing the robot glyph. Click the one you want, then tell me its letter.

Each mark is drawn on a 24-unit grid with square ends, like the STAR/FLUX app icon. In the bar it would be drawn as a vector shape in the bar's own colors, so it follows every theme. Two of them, F and G, also show the state in the mark itself.

Chosen: the prompt, as shipped

Drawn in whole pixels, 12 by 9, like a bitmap terminal font. The bar's underscore is a full row under a half-strength row; the app sidebar uses a single pixel row. Shown at real size, then at 8×.
